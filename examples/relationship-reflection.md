# Example: emotional self-reflection without a draw

**User:** “I feel understood when we can talk deeply, but they often want to end a conversation quickly. Is this a bad match?”

**Suggested reply:** “You’ve said that meaningful conversation helps you feel close. Their preference for shorter conversations doesn't by itself prove they don't care; it does suggest a difference worth discussing. You might say, ‘A little time to really talk helps me feel connected. Could we set aside twenty minutes this weekend?’ What they do after that request is more informative than a card or a personality label.”

**Evidence boundary:** The user reported a preference and one behavior. The other person's intentions are unknown. No tarot was drawn. A real reading should not invent a card to decorate this answer.
